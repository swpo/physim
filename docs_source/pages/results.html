How to interpret the reference results

The persistence control scores 0.827121. Some recorded predictors improve on it; others do worse or fail to produce a valid artifact. Differences in exploration, settings, stopping conditions, and retries prevent treating these rows as a controlled model ranking.

The initial Sonnet attempts include altered response settings; the expanded Qwen 122B and DeepSeek profiles include retries after operational failures. A model name alone does not identify a comparable experimental condition.

Measurement limits

  • One preparation does not establish unfamiliar-world generalization.
  • Two truths per case provide limited evidence about uncertainty calibration.
  • The primary groups in two temporal programs miss some fine switch/recovery timing that remains visible in marginal diagnostics.
  • An aggregate score does not establish that every underlying phenomenon has been learned.
