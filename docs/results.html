Evidence and current results

These historical control and model tables cover the prepared p4g2_044 reference. BF and XV now provide two additional validated laboratories with initial model pilots.

The reference's 15 programs cover pulse responses, intervention history, and probe placement. Every native model prediction uses 64 forecast members and is compared with two retained independent truth realizations per case. Lower primary joint energy is better.

Additional BF and XV pilots

Each new laboratory has 11 programs and one completed DeepSeek V4 Flash pilot. BF scored 0.49675 against initial persistence at 0.46001; XV scored 0.18418 against persistence at 0.42990. Lower is better within a suite; scores across suites are not directly comparable. Both predictors passed the interface. One pilot per preparation does not establish a model ranking or recovery of the underlying physics. The scientific report includes intervention figures, mechanism controls, settings, and interpretation limits.

How to interpret the reference results

The persistence control scores 0.827121. Some recorded predictors improve on it; others do worse or fail to produce a valid artifact. Differences in exploration, settings, stopping conditions, and retries prevent treating these rows as a controlled model ranking.

The initial Sonnet attempts include altered response settings; the expanded Qwen 122B and DeepSeek profiles include retries after operational failures. A model name alone does not identify a comparable experimental condition.

Measurement limits

  • One preparation does not establish unfamiliar-world generalization.
  • Two truths per case provide limited evidence about uncertainty calibration.
  • The primary groups in two temporal programs miss some fine switch/recovery timing that remains visible in marginal diagnostics.
  • An aggregate score does not establish that every underlying phenomenon has been learned.
