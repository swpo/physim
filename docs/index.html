Overview

Learning physical systems through experiments

Physim studies whether AI agents can learn unfamiliar physics through experiments.

Built with Prime Intellect’s Verifiers, the evaluation environment gives agents a virtual laboratory: they choose experiments, use their observations to build a predictive model, and are tested on predictions of new experiments. Simulation lets these investigations be repeated and scaled without the throughput constraints of physical laboratory work.

To supply interesting worlds, Blobkit provides tools for searching the space of equations governing primitive, interacting fields. Motion, binding, and collective behavior emerge from the resulting dynamics. The package supports simulation and evolutionary search at scale, customizable measurements, and harvesting worlds together with the recipes that generated them.
