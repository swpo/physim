**23 October 2026 — P7 retains its internal pattern, but does not restore it after this interior pulse**

P7 is a persistent pattern in an eight-field simulation. Its earlier evaluation supported a localized apparent individual with a recognizable internal arrangement: a curled A1 stripe, a low-A1 bay and an adjacent A0 band. This experiment asked whether that specific arrangement returns after erasure, and whether a surrounding boundary region contributes to that return.

The six cases start from one accepted checkpoint. Pulses reset two activator fields in fixed regions while retaining the other six fields. Every case runs for 250 simulation units under the same new noise sequence, with 101 saved observations. Recovery requires the original arrangement in every saved observation during the final 50 units. Merely regaining activity or retaining an enclosing outline does not satisfy that endpoint.

| Case | Pulse | Standing evaluator's finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S | None | Original feature retained throughout the observed window. |
| B | Boundary region | Censored: the feature extends beyond the fixed interior readout. |
| C | Exterior region | Original feature retained throughout the observed window. |
| D | Interior | Activity regrows into a different three-arm A1 pattern with dispersed A0. Original feature not recovered. |
| DB | Interior and boundary region | Censored: the original apparent individual's continuation becomes ambiguous amid spot reorganization. |
| DC | Interior and exterior region | Different three-arm A1 pattern and dispersed A0. Original feature not recovered. |

In D and DC the wider view still shows an apparent enclosure at the original location, but the internal pattern is different in all 21 late observations. This is evidence against restoration of the specified feature in this pulse/window. B and DB cannot be counted as clean selective failures. The required combination—S/B/C retention, D/DC recovery and DB nonrecovery—was not observed, so the proposed conditional boundary contribution is not established.

All six executions completed validly. The standing evaluator inspected every interior and context observation and checked joint artifact, source, schedule and opaque RNG metadata. The evidence contains eight-bit fixed-interior activator values and coarse four-bit full-domain context, not complete raw field trajectories. This is one selected candidate and one paired noise branch. Equal-area boundary/exterior masks differ in state, gradients, distance and actual pulse dose; retained fields may carry chemical memory. Later recovery, numerical convergence, autonomy and copying remain unestablished.

**Decision: stop this fixed recovery/conditional-boundary claim as a completed limited negative with censored boundary cases.** P7's earlier visible-organization and sampled-persistence finding remains supported under the unchanged rubric. No automatic rerun, longer window or altered endpoint follows from this result.
