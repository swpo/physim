# Program committee

The committee is the lab's peer review and funding body. It runs in the coordinating
session. For each sprint report it:

1. assembles the thread's report and evidence packs from the researcher's branch;
2. asks three judges with different lenses to score the claims against
   [RUBRIC.md](../RUBRIC.md):
   - **biologist**: which ladder rungs are really supported, from the images;
   - **physicist-skeptic**: numerics, triviality, robustness; reruns a pack;
   - **curator**: novelty against the literature and other threads, use of
     non-diffusive operators, value as a PhySim world;
3. has the **director** write the funding decision (continue / pivot / merge / stop),
   the single most valuable next experiment, and cross-pollination pointers to
   other threads;
4. sends the review to the researcher and records it in `reviews/`, then updates
   [LEADERBOARD.md](LEADERBOARD.md).

Funding here means attention and compute. A stopped thread's researcher is
reassigned to a new direction chosen by the director.
