## Nearest published work to what was just established

**Fed vortices that divide (c53).** The closest published "self-replicating vortices" are Marcus, Pei, Jiang &
Hassanzadeh, PRL 111, 084501 (2013) (opened, preprint PDF): in linearly stable, rotating, stratified 3D shear flows a
baroclinic critical layer draws energy from the background shear, rolls up into a vortex, and that vortex excites new
critical layers and vortices, filling space with a regular lattice ("zombie vortices"). Relation: a **variant** in name
only. Their replication is excitation of new vortices at fixed lattice sites, powered by a global shear, with no
population of individuals that move, die, fuse or carry a kind; c53's vortices split by neighbour strain into two of
their own sign, feed on a local consumable fuel, and fuse. I found no 2D forced-turbulence paper that treats vortex
fission and fusion as births and deaths of a population (the vortex-gas literature, e.g. topographic turbulence, treats
vortices as shielded, conserved objects). The c53 gain term (k Y s - mu) omega is not a standard fluid forcing, which is
worth stating in any write-up.

**Predators attached to prey (c52).** Froese, Ikegami & Virgo, "The behavior-based hypercycle: from parasitic reaction to
symbiotic behavior", ALIFE 13 (2012) (opened, PDF): a second Gray-Scott autocatalyst C that feeds directly on the
primary autocatalyst B; the B spot moves away from C and C follows as a "tail", so the spot-tail pair is self-motile and
colonizes new nutrient faster. Relation to c52's claim: **variant**. The structure is the same (a consumer riding its
food body as a tail, one prey per consumer), which is what my pairtest found in c52's world (88% of predator bodies touch
prey; 302 of 306 long-lived predators touch one prey lineage for life). c52 adds conserved, non-food-made predator
matter, condensation driven by digestion, and predator division that is mostly not simultaneous with prey division (3/36).
Froese et al. read the pair as the unit that moves and is selected, which supports reviewers who treat c52's I and D as
the pair's. Meredith, Moerman, Groenewold, Chiu, Kegel, van Blaaderen & Zarzar, Nature Chemistry (2020) (opened, Penn
State release; the PubMed page would not load): oil droplets of two chemistries chase each other by non-reciprocal oil
exchange through micelles; clusters rotate, move in spirals and split into smaller clusters. Relation: **variant** for
the hunting (non-reciprocal chemical chasing, as in c46's written fuel-depletion taxis); the release says nothing about
droplets eating, growing or dividing, so the ecology (eating + reproduction) is not there.

**Heredity and selection in dissipative structures (c49, c53; c48's negatives).** Bunin & Rivoire, "Evolutionary features
in a minimal physical system: diversity, selection, growth, inheritance, and adaptation", PNAS (2025), arXiv:2411.03843
(opened): reaction-diffusion Langevin equations for assemblies of building blocks; compositional states expand in space,
are inherited through correlated compositions, and are selected by stability/productivity and competition for space.
Relation: **variant** at the level of principle (heredity by self-copying composition, no template), but their units
are spatial domains of states, not discrete, dividing, moving bodies. c49's and c53's kinds (a label made only where the
body burns or eats, carried in its own fluid) are closer to "composition that is carried and copied by the body"; I found
no published fluid-dynamical body that carries a self-copied label through fission.

## Published dynamics the ledger has not tried

1. **Barkley's model of transitional pipe flow (puffs that split and decay).** Barkley, "Simplifying the complexity of pipe
   flow", PRE 84, 016309 (2011), arXiv:1101.4125 (opened): q_t + U q_x = q(u + r - 1 - (r + delta)(q - 1)^2) + q_xx,
   u_t + U u_x = eps1 (1 - u) - eps2 u q - u_x (eps1 0.04, eps2 0.2, delta 0.1); a stochastic/map version captures
   metastable localized puffs, puff decay and puff splitting with superexponential lifetimes and a directed-percolation
   transition. This is the cleanest published case of localized structures with stochastic births (splits) and deaths
   (decays) whose rates cross at a critical Reynolds number: a ready-made birth-death process for C:open-ended and
   for heredity tests, with an advective (non-Laplacian) term. Untried in the ledger (no class or contribution mentions
   it). See the question published with this check.
2. **Zombie-vortex replication (Marcus et al. 2013)** needs 3D stratified rotating shear; too expensive here, noted only.
3. **Bunin-Rivoire compositional inheritance** could be grafted onto an existing body class (several self-copied
   labels with a reaction network among them instead of c48's two exclusive labels), giving many kinds without a written
   fitness parameter. Not a new class; noted for C:open-ended.
