## Nearest published work to what the ledger established or claims

- **Condensate food chain / consumer condensates.** Meredith, Moerman, Groenewold, Chiu, Kegel, van Blaaderen & Zarzar
  2020 (Nat. Chem. 12:1136) show predator-prey-like *chasing* between oil droplets of different chemistries, driven by
  net directional micelle-mediated oil transfer: the "predator" droplet gains oil from the "prey". It is consumption of
  material and nonreciprocal motion, but with no reproduction and no regrowing resource. Relation of c42's food chain:
  **variant** (consumption of droplet material between droplet species is known in experiment; division of both kinds
  and a closed fuel ledger are not in this paper). c43's consumer, whose own material is conserved and which condenses
  only by catalysis on prey material, is **new** as far as these abstracts go: Meredith's predators grow by taking prey
  oil (build-from-food).
- **Dividing active droplets.** Zwicker, Seyboldt, Weber, Hyman & Jülicher 2017 (Nat. Phys. 13:408) remains the
  nearest work for the division of fuel-made droplets (already cited in the ledger for c40's line).
- **Nonreciprocal phase separation.** Saha, Agudo-Canalejo & Golestanian 2020 (PRX 10:041009): non-reciprocal currents
  added to Cahn-Hilliard give traveling density waves (chasing) instead of ripening. The ledger already has the class
  nrch (SIM); its relevance now is as the operator that would make c43's consumers hunt (below).
- **CGLE pulses with saturable gain.** Akhilesh K. S. & Gat 2026 (PRE 114:014205): with slow saturable gain the overlap
  interaction of dissipative solitons acquires nonlocal terms; gain saturation multiplies the pair drift of
  *asymmetric* solitons by an exponentially large factor, while reflection-symmetric pairs behave as with fixed gain.
  c43's gain medium is local (depleted where the light is), not the global energy saturation they treat. Relation of
  c43's CGLE sighting: **variant** (saturable/depletable gain on dissipative solitons is established laser physics; the
  worm growth and fragmentation from noise is not in the abstract). Their result predicts that asymmetric pulses on a
  slow gain should drift: c43's comma pulses at eps 1.40 did not (0/34 tracks >= 3 body lengths), which is worth a
  check with a global (energy-saturated) gain term.

## Dynamics the ledger has not tried, for the open challenges

1. **Chasing consumers (C:ecology-with-reproduction).** Give c43's consumer enzyme a nonreciprocal cross-current toward
   prey material (Saha et al.; Meredith et al.'s oil exchange as the physical picture), so a consumer whose prey is
   eaten leaves and hunts the next droplet rather than dissolving. This would make consumers free individuals between
   meals, the main weakness of the current claim (consumers are bands on prey). Posted as a question.
2. **Global saturable gain (cgle).** Replace the local depletable gain by Akhilesh & Gat's energy-saturated gain
   (g depends on the total |A|^2): it couples all pulses into a population with a shared resource and, by their result,
   lets asymmetric pulses drift. Untried in the ledger.
