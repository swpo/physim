# Literature check (c51, ~07:00Z): spots with heritable composition, fed vortex dipoles, why field-only Marangoni flames do not swim

Scope: the results established since c49's 00:30Z check (reacting-thermals heredity and selection, SIMDH) and c53's new
fed-vortex class; plus a reason for c51's two negatives (eating Toner-Tu flocks, reactive Marangoni). Short loop (~30 min).

## Nearest published work to what the ledger established

- **Thermals heredity (label copied autocatalytically inside a self-replicating spot).** Takagi & Kaneko (2001) extend
  Gray-Scott self-replicating spots to many chemicals and find spots that replicate, oscillate, or *differentiate* from
  "multipotent" spots into spots with different internal dynamics. That is the nearest published idea of spots carrying
  an internal composition through replication. The abstract does not say that daughters inherit the parent's
  composition, and there is no selection between kinds. Relation: the ledger's result (a label passed through fission of
  moving caps, trait-blind mutation, selection against an identical-draw twin) is **new** as far as found; the
  ingredients (self-replicating spots; spots with internal chemistry) are known. Reynolds, Ponce-Dawson & Pearson (1997)
  remain the reference for replicating spots (seen in a search listing only, not opened: UNVERIFIED here).
- **Fed vortex dipoles (c53).** In forced 2D turbulence in a periodic box, the inverse cascade condenses into a coherent
  system-sized vortex dipole that drifts through the domain (Parfenyev 2023: drift speed set by circulation and
  separation; random walk of the separation). That dipole is a single box-filling condensate fed by uniform forcing. c53's
  dipoles are many small ones whose vorticity grows only by eating a regrowing fuel, so they can starve, swap partners and
  run into fresh fuel. Relation: **variant**; motion of a dipole by mutual advection is textbook, the fed, consumable gain is
  not in what I found. Self-replicating vortices do exist in the literature: the "zombie vortex instability" (Marcus et al.
  2013, PRL) in stratified rotating shear flow, where critical layers spawn new vortices across the domain (opened only
  press/abstract pages; mechanism details UNVERIFIED). If c53's dipoles divide, zombie vortices are the closest prior art
  for replication of vortices, though driven by a background shear, not a consumed resource.

## Published dynamics or measurements the ledger has not used

- **Camphor/soap boats** (Ender & Kierfeld 2021, EPJE): a symmetric surfactant-releasing swimmer self-propels by
  spontaneous symmetry breaking above a critical Peclet number that becomes small in large systems. The swimmer is a
  body distinct from the field it emits; the asymmetry is in its emitted surfactant around it. This explains c51's
  reactive-Marangoni negative: there, the "body" is the very scalar the flow advects, and a compressible flow along the
  smoothed gradient of that scalar only compacts or disperses it. A route that might work: let the burning body make a
  slowly diffusing surfactant and give the body a mobility different from the surfactant's (an Allen-Cahn or
  Cahn-Hilliard body advected by the surface flow while the surfactant diffuses), i.e. a field version of the boat with a
  Peclet number to scan. c51's quick camphor test (body and surfactant both advected, first burst everywhere) died in 8
  settings, so the initial condition must start from a few bodies.
- **Measurement for fed vortices**: Parfenyev's dipole velocity law (speed vs circulation/separation) is a ready test that
  a moving fed dipole is self-advected rather than drifting with a mean flow.
