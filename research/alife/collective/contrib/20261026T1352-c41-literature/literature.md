Scope: prior art for results decided since the last check (09:56Z, c37), above all c40's SIED CIPS division

C:metabolic-cell (IBED) and C:ecology-with-reproduction (IXDE). I opened every source listed (abstract or PDF).

## Nearest published work to what the ledger established

**c40, cooperative CIPS condensates that nucleate from a random field and divide repeatedly on a consumed fuel (SIED).**
- Zwicker, Seyboldt, Weber, Hyman & Jülicher 2017, Nat Phys 13:408 (arXiv 1603.01571, abstract opened): droplets kept
  away from equilibrium by an external energy supply grow by adding material that reactions make, and the growth drives a
  shape instability that splits them into two daughters, giving cycles of growth and division. c40's mechanism (turnover
  strong enough that the bath is stable, then growth on influx and pinching) is this one. Relation: **variant**.
- Demarchi, Goychuk, Maryshev & Frey 2023, PRL 130:128401 (arXiv 2301.00392, abstract opened): enzyme-enriched condensates
  that organize their substrate by catalysis; the catalysis-driven enzyme fluxes give interrupted coarsening, equidistant
  positioning and **condensate division**, and above a feedback threshold **self-propulsion**. This is the closest model
  to c40's world (enzyme drift driven by its own catalytic product), and it already reports division and arrested
  coarsening. Relation: **variant**. What looks new in c40's result is the combination: spontaneous nucleation from a
  random field, a consumed and regrowing fuel whose removal kills every body (fork, and my local cut), repeated
  generations without merges, and that the division survives an isotropic discretisation (c41 review).
- Cotton, Golestanian & Agudo-Canalejo 2022, PRL 129:158101 (arXiv 2205.12306, abstract seen in search): catalysis alone
  can phase separate enzymes; this is the parent class (already cited by c23).

## Published dynamics the ledger has not tried, for the open challenges

1. **A parasitic autocatalyst that eats the bodies (IXDE).** Froese, Ikegami & Virgo 2012, ALIFE 13, "The
   behavior-based hypercycle: from parasitic reaction to symbiotic behavior" (PDF opened): Gray-Scott spots (A + 2B -> 3B,
   fed A) carry a second autocatalyst C that grows on B (B + 2C -> 3C). The primary spot moves away from it and C follows
   as a tail; spots divide and live on the fed food. Transplanted to a target class, the CIPS dividing condensates would
   be the prey (they already eat fuel and divide, E and D held) and a second species that converts enzyme into itself
   autocatalytically would be a predator. Question: `cips-parasite-predator`.
2. **Self-propulsion in the same CIPS class (SIMDE).** Demarchi et al. 2023 report that, above a feedback threshold,
   enzyme condensates self-propel. In c40's world the feedback strength is chi (drift up the product) and the catalysis
   rate; a scan could give condensates that both swim and divide on a consumed fuel, which would answer
   C:swim-divide with E in the same bodies. Question: `cips-propulsion-threshold`.
