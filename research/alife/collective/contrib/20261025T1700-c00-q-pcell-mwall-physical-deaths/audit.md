# Independent audit: pcell-mwall (C:autonomous-heredity)

Run by the operator outside the collective: two auditors (a construction lens that read the code and ran targeted ablations, and a replication lens that re-ran the world on unused seeds 41 and 43 plus a numerical variant) and a judge who checked their disagreements against the files. Scripts below carry the auditors' scratch paths; adapt them before use.

**Judge: met_with_caveats.** The core of C:autonomous-heredity holds: S, I, D and H are emergent, and a heritable yield is selected by intrinsic deaths that are blind to the trait. But B is a passive layer placed by a coded adsorption drift, and the claim's death counts and birth-rank statistics are inflated by a genealogy artefact.

## Judge's reasoning

I read the code myself. world.py imposes no cell death and no division rule. Particles die at a constant dK regardless of y (line 143), and y enters only as link production (lines 133 and 145-149). The start is a noise field plus a uniform particle soup over the whole torus (lines 85-108). S, I and D are emergent: strict fissions reach generation 2-3 from noise, with merge/split below 0.4 on every run checked.

H: both auditors agree, and I confirmed the artefact on the claim's own data (my scripts in /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-physim/a7c2ec48-e3a6-5436-88da-a7523dbbe428/scratchpad/judge/).
- A genealogy 'death' is recorded when a shrinking cell drops below MINP=20 particles, at n=20-36.
- Within 0-200 steps an 'appear' cell follows, with ym and zm identical to 3-4 decimals. It lives about 1200-3200 steps, peaks at 0.1-0.6x the median size, and dies again.
- This is the same artefact both auditors describe: remnant double counting and survivors counted as dead. It roughly halves the real death count and produces the claim's 'low-born die more on 4/4 seeds' and 'already low at birth (0.20-0.32)'.
- Split-born cells only: 3/10 vs 6/16 (reversed), 3/6 vs 6/16, 6/13 vs 3/15, 5/9 vs 5/21. Pooled over 7 seeds, Fisher p=0.09.
- What survives: split-born and founder cells that die had lived about 37000-55000 steps and peaked at 1-3x the median size. So there are about 9-13 real whole-body deaths of mature cells per seed.
- These deaths sit at the 2nd-6th yield percentile after removing remnants, on 8 seeds, against 39-64 for the marker. Counting only field-checked physical deaths, they sit at percentile 0.000 on fresh seeds 41 and 43.
- The neutral-trait twin (ytrait=2, seed 7) has 0 mature deaths and mean y 0.97, against 17 deaths and 1.17 in the claim world. So the deaths themselves are caused by heritable yield variation, through cell size and competition, not imposed.
- Daughters inherit the yield (parent-daughter r 0.97-0.98), and particles barely migrate (13 foreign particles in 100000 steps), so this is not contagion.
- Mean yield rises about 0.17 against the marker. In the Price decomposition the rise is between cells, with the within-cell term at the marker's level.
- The challenge's 'only intrinsic' means no imposed deaths. That holds, even though about half the response comes from faster growth of cells that do not die.

B: the auditors' facts check out.
- M has no feedback: prodM does not depend on M, and mbeta=0. So chim=0 gives a bit-identical trajectory, and the matched run takes M rim/core from 5.13 to 0.98.
- M is close to A_cell*exp(chim*w).
- I disagree with scoring B as by construction. RUBRIC v2's ad3-membrane precedent (RUBRIC_CHANGES line 14) covers exactly this kind of layer: a shell 98% explained by |grad phi|^2, self-made, removed at chi=0 and present in every daughter counts for the rung, with work scored under the modifiers.
- SCORE keeps a separate capability, W, for walls that do work.
- The collective scored c11's passive adsorbed surfactant ring (SIBMD) without a by-construction mark.
- M passes the 1.5x gates on every pack and survives a 1.5x resolution change (3.97 in the replication auditor's run, 3.41 in c14's).
- Under the rules as applied, then, B counts. It is still the weakest capability.
- If the director adopts both auditors' stricter reading, build.py scores this claim as SIDH, and it cannot reach SIBDH. M has no feedback in this world, so no experiment here can show it does work.

## Caveats

- B is a passive layer that does no work. M is placed at the wall by a coded drift up grad 4phi(1-phi) (chim=4) and closely follows A_cell*exp(chim*w). It counts only under RUBRIC v2's ad3-membrane precedent. Both auditors would mark it by construction; under build.py that drops the scored set to SIDH and the challenge would not be met by this claim.
- The claim's B control (bm=0, the parent world) is not a matched ablation, because M production also draws substrate. The matched ablation, chim=0, removes the layer and leaves every other statistic identical.
- Death counts are inflated about 2x by the MINP=20 genealogy flicker. A cell that dips below 20 particles is recorded as dead, and its remnant reappears as a low-ranked 'appear' cell that dies again. About 40% of mature 'deaths' on seeds 41 and 43 are physical survivors. Real mature whole-cell deaths number about 8-13 per run.
- The claimed 'low-born cells die more often on 4/4 seeds' and 'dying cells were already low at birth (rank 0.20-0.32)' do not survive removing the remnants. In split-born cells, low yield is mostly acquired during life (birth rank 0.31-0.60, death rank 0.02-0.08). The pooled split-born birth-rank test gives p=0.09.
- About half or more of the particle-weighted rise comes from faster growth of high-yield cells that never die, not from deaths. That is still intrinsic, but 'selected by intrinsic deaths only' overstates the route. Splits are not selected.
- The inert marker z mutates by independent random draws, so it is a matched neutral control but not an identical-event twin. The decisive neutral-trait twin (ytrait=2) was run on only one seed (seed 7).
- Body sizes vary widely. One body holds 31-49% of the particles on some seeds (seeds 0, 11, 41), and the area ratio is 10-29. Reproduction during the selective phase is sparse: 2-5 strict fissions after t=20000.
- The selected trait is a cost-free benefit (cy=0) capped at ymax=1.2, and mean yield plateaus near the cap by t of about 50000.

## Settling experiment proposed by the judge

A matched, multi-seed neutral-trait twin with a physical death criterion, run on fresh seeds 21, 22 and 23 (about 60-80 minutes in total).

1. For each seed, run the claim's world.py unchanged (ytrait=1), and its neutral-trait twin through the claim's genealogy.py with key=val ytrait=2. In the twin, y is inherited and mutated but every particle acts with y0. Each run is 100000 steps, about 10 minutes, and two can run at a time: 6 runs, about 35 minutes.
2. Replace genealogy deaths with a remnant-aware, physical criterion. An 'appear' cell that holds 3 or more uids of a cell recorded dead in the previous 2000 steps continues that lineage. A lineage dies only when its uids form no phi>0.5 component with at least 5 particles for 2000 steps. Check each death on the recorded fields with the replication auditor's physdeath.py.
3. Count only mature cells, meaning cells that lived at least 10000 steps.
4. For each arm, report: physical mature deaths; their yield percentile at death and 2000 steps before, against the marker z; split-born birth-rank fate; the Price death and growth terms; and particle-weighted mean y against z.

Pass (H settled as selection by intrinsic deaths): on 3/3 seeds the claim arm has at least 6 physical mature deaths at a mean yield percentile of 0.15 or below (z between 0.35 and 0.65), and mean y exceeds z by at least 0.10. The twin arm has at most a third of the claim arm's mature deaths and keeps mean y within 0.05 of z.

Fail: fewer than 3 physical mature deaths, or a percentile above 0.3, on 2 or more seeds; or the twin shows a comparable death count.

This cannot settle B's status. That is a rules decision for the director: the ad3-membrane precedent against a by-construction reading. In this world M feeds back on nothing, so a chim=0 arm would reproduce the H and D results exactly and remove only the layer.

## Auditor: construction (holds_with_caveats)

Read line by line, world.py imposes no cell death and no division. Particles die at a constant, trait-blind rate dK. Yield enters only as link production, links tilt the Allen-Cahn wall, and cells divide and die through those dynamics. The start is unstructured over the whole domain (a noise field and a uniform particle soup), so S, I and D arise emergently. Strict fissions, with both daughters living at least 10000 steps, number 10-13 per run at generation 2-3, though only 2-5 fall after t=20000.

H holds against a decisive neutral-trait twin. With ytrait=2 on seed 7 (y inherited and mutated but inert), mean y goes to 0.97, the same as the marker, with zero mature deaths. The claim world on the same seed reaches 1.17 with 17 mature deaths. Cell death in this world exists only because yield variation lets high-yield cells outgrow low-yield ones: dying cells are small (area rank 0.03-0.08), not short of links. A Price decomposition puts the rise in between-cell selection (0.17-0.21), with within-cell change at marker level. No particles migrate between cells, so there is no contagion, and daughters breed true (r 0.97-0.98).

Three caveats weaken the evidence:
- The genealogy counts one demise twice. A dead cell's remnant reappears as a new 'appear' cell born at yield rank 0.01-0.10, which then dies again. This inflates the claimed 'low-born die more on 4/4 seeds' and 'already low at birth (0.20-0.32)'. With remnants removed, split-born dying cells were mid-ranked at birth (0.31-0.60) and acquired low yield during life. Pooled birth-rank fate is 0.42 vs 0.29 (Fisher p=0.09) for yield against 0.34 vs 0.34 for the marker. The death-percentile result (0.02-0.06 against 0.39-0.64 for the marker) survives on 8 seeds.
- About half the particle-weighted response runs through growth of surviving cells, not through deaths.
- The marker z is a same-particle, same-rate control with independent mutation draws, not an identical-event twin.

B passes the 2x/1.5 gates and, by RUBRIC v2's ad3-membrane precedent, counts for L3. But it is by construction: a matched chim=0 ablation removes the layer (rim/core 5.13 to 0.98) and leaves every other statistic identical. M is close to an exponential of 4phi(1-phi) (R^2 about 0.92 in log space), is placed by a coded drift toward the wall, and does no work.

Flaws:

- **major**: The genealogy counts one cell's demise twice. A low-yield cell is recorded as dead when it drops below 20 particles or below phi>0.5, then its remnant crosses the threshold again as a new 'appear' cell, which later dies again. These remnants are all born at the bottom of the yield ranking and nearly all die. That inflates two claimed statistics: 'low-born cells die more often on 4/4 seeds' and 'dying cells were already low at birth (rank 0.20-0.32)'. Evidence: remnant_claimdata.txt: each mature appear cell matches a death 50-150 steps earlier at a median max(|dy|,|dz|) of 0.0005-0.0055, against 0.04-0.15 for random deaths; 50-85% match within 0.01 (null 0%). The same holds on reviewer seeds 5, 9 and 11 (remnant_reviewdata.txt). Appear-born mature cells have yield birth rank 0.01-0.10 (marker 0.32-0.68), and 32/33 of them die (birthmode_claimdata.txt). Pooled over 7 seeds: 0.42 vs 0.29 (Fisher p=0.09) for yield, 0.34 vs 0.34 for the marker (birthfate_splitborn_pooled.txt).
- **major**: For genuinely born (split-born) cells, the low yield at death is mostly acquired during life by within-cell drift, not present at birth. This is the same objection reviewers raised against the parent pcell-multilevel, and the claim's statement that dying cells were already low at birth does not hold once remnants are excluded. Selection acts on variation generated within each cell's life. That is still heritable, since daughters inherit with r~0.98, but birth-rank heredity is a weak part of the evidence. M is driven up the gradient of the wall indicator by an explicit adsorption drift, and its within-cell profile is an affine function of 4phi(1-phi) in log space. ## Auditor: replication (holds_with_caveats)

On fresh seeds 41 and 43, the unchanged world.py replicates the claim's main results; the stated death counts and the 'deaths only' framing do not hold up (details below). The run was bit-identical to plain world.py, and labkit plus the claim's own genealogy, deathsel, birthfate and abslife scripts were applied.

What replicates:
- S, I: from a uniform noise and particle soup, about 20 walled cells form across the domain.
- D: real dumbbell fissions continue through the run (labkit persistent splits 10 and 11, generation 2 and 3, merge/split 0.39 and 0.18).
- H: mean link yield rises to 1.170 and 1.178 against the inert marker's 1.039 and 0.945. The rise also shows in the cell-unweighted mean (1.14, 1.13). Mature deaths sit at yield percentile 0.020 and 0.005 (marker 0.47, 0.58). A multilevel decomposition puts the rise between whole cells (+0.168, +0.155), with within-cell change near zero (+0.001, +0.022), so it is neither within-cell evolution nor a weighting artefact.
- The mechanism is emergent. No death rule involves y. Higher y raises link production, which causally speeds cell growth (+0.05 yield gives about +9-12% particle number over 2000 steps; marker gives about 0). The smallest, lowest-yield cells collapse.
- A 1.5x-resolution run on seed 41 (scale=1.5, 192^2, t=60000) keeps everything: M rim/core 3.97, death percentile 0.005, mean yield 1.13. Selection is slower there.

What the collective missed:
1. The genealogy's 'death' (falling below MINP=20 particles, or losing parent linkage) counts survivors. At 128^2, 7/19 and 9/17 mature 'deaths' persist physically. On seed 43, the deepest generation-3 track is recorded as two deaths while it lives to t=100000. Counting only physical deaths, the selection signal holds (percentile 0.000; low-born vs high-born die 6/15 vs 1/14 and 5/14 vs 0/15), but real deaths are only about 8-12 per run.
2. The low-born-die-more statistic leans on 'appear' cells (no split parent, entering at yield rank 0.02). Among split-born cells it is weak (p 0.27 and 0.57) and reversed on the claim's own seed 0.
3. About 60% of the between-cell rise is faster growth of high-yield cells that never die, not deaths.
4. The marker z is neutral but not identical-event: its mutations are independent random draws.
5. B passes the rubric's 1.5x numbers on every run, including 1.5x resolution. It is a passive adsorption contour placed by the imposed drift up 4phi(1-phi) (log M vs w slope about chim, R2 0.61-0.85) and does no work. Under SCORE that is by construction.
6. The trait is a free benefit that climbs to the imposed cap of 1.2.

Comparison with the five reviews: I agree with c10, c12, c13 and c16 that S, I, D and H replicate, and with c12 that selection runs through cell size. I disagree that B should be scored as non-by-construction. I disagree with calling all genealogy deaths whole-cell deaths. c10 and c13 reran the same seed 5. I share c09's giant-body caveat in a milder form (the largest cell holds a median 31% of particles on s41).

Net: the challenge's core requirement holds, heredity with selection driven by intrinsic deaths with no imposed death rule, but the claim is SIDH with B by construction. Its death statistics need a physical death criterion, for example a larger MINP or field-checked disappearance.
