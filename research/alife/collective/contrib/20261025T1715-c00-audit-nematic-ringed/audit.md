# Independent audit: ringed active-nematic rods (C:swim-divide)

Run by the operator outside the collective: two auditors (a construction lens that read the code and ran targeted ablations, and a replication lens that re-ran the world on unused seeds 41 and 43 plus a numerical variant) and a judge who checked their disagreements against the files. Scripts below carry the auditors' scratch paths; adapt them before use.

**Judge: not_met.** The rods form from noise, swim, divide, and carry a real surfactant ring, but the ring is a passive coating made mostly by the medium and imposed by the code, so B holds by construction and is not scored: SIMD is reached, SIBMD is not.

## Judge's reasoning

I checked every point of disagreement and every critical or major flaw against the code and the auditors' files. I re-ran no simulations, only short analyses of existing frames.

**What I confirmed in the code** (/home/user/physim/research/alife/collective/contrib/20261025T1141-c11-active-nematic-ringed/world.py):
- Lines 101-110: the surfactant s drifts up grad w, with w = 1 - phi^2. That is 4c(1-c) for c = (1+phi)/2, which is exactly the rubric's example of a band-pass readout of the body.
- The source is ks*(1+phi)/2 everywhere.
- s never enters the phi, Q or velocity updates, so the ring does no work and chis=0 gives identical bodies.

**Why B holds by construction.** RUBRIC v2 caps such a layer at Weak unless it does work, which this one does not. The only exception is the quasi-equilibrium clause, which needs a self-made species.
- The precedent for that clause, ad3-membrane, has a source k u c^2. In a bath at phi = -0.95 that source is negligible, so its shell really was self-made (/home/user/physim/research/alife/threads/active-droplets/worlds/ad3-membrane/world.py).
- Here the chemostat holds the bath at phi of -0.59 to -0.70, where the source still runs at 15-20% of ks. The bath therefore makes 63-82% of all surfactant (construction/r1_seed2_source.txt).
- Far-field s is 0.17-0.21, the same as the rod interior at about 0.21-0.22 (replication/audit_seed41.json, global_raw_s).
- I re-analysed the three-way source-split frames (construction/r6_srcsplit3_seed2/frames.npz) using the signed distance to the phi=0 contour. Surfactant made only in the far bath forms a ring by itself: rim/core 2.18, rim/out 2.16. That is a stronger ring than the rods' own surfactant gives (rim/core 1.52, rim/out 5.09). In the ring pixels, only 36% of s was made by rods (r6_rimshare.txt).
- So the ring is an adsorption image of whatever surfactant is present. The individual's own production does not decide it.
- Both auditors reached B = by construction independently. None of the six collective reviews flagged it, and four of them scored B as Supported.
- Under SCORE.md, a by-construction capability is recorded but not scored. The scored set is therefore SIMD, which is the parent's set, and C:swim-divide (SIBMD) is not met.

**One correction to the construction auditor.** It says the rod-made copy "fails the gate (1.26)". That figure is labkit's centroid-radial metric, which the same auditor calls wrong for rods. By signed distance on the same frames, the rod-made copy passes, though only just (1.52 / 5.09). The critical flaw still stands, because medium-made surfactant alone forms the ring. But whether the rods' own product would pass on its own is borderline rather than a clear failure. That is what the proposed experiment settles.

**Where the two auditors disagree:**
- **Overall verdict.** The construction auditor says "fails" and the replication auditor says "holds with caveats". The replication auditor's "holds" covers S, I, M and D, and it also says B should be recorded but not scored. So on the challenge itself they agree.
- **M.** I side mostly with the replication auditor and keep M, with caveats.
  - labkit's body length is sqrt(mean area) (/home/user/physim/research/alife/labkit/metrics.py line 800), the collective's standing convention.
  - Under it, 3 to 9 tracks per run reach 3 body lengths on every dx=1 run.
  - Measured in rod long-axis lengths, 1 or 2 tracks per run still reach 3 on 4 of the 5 dx=1 runs (maxima 3.14 to 4.29).
  - The construction auditor's claim that the tail disappears at dt/2 comes from one seed cut at t=900. The replication auditor's dt/2 run on seed 41 has 9 tracks at 3 or more labkit body lengths, and matched tracks travel the same path (ratio 1.03).
  - Rods isolated from their neighbours keep their speed (r8_isolate_seed2.txt), so the motion is self-propelled.
- **dt/2 record.** Both auditors are right that the parent's dt/2 control was mislabelled. labkit's --dt is "for reporting; the world's own numerics are untouched" (run.py lines 36-37). The construction auditor's r2 run, with the world's dt unchanged, reproduces pack_dt2's component counts exactly over 31 of 31 frames. A genuine dt/2 converges for nucleation, splits and speed.

**What I saw.** ring_seed0.png shows a bright s band on the phi=0 contour of every rod, and chis=0 removes it. In r6 radial.png, far-bath-made s also peaks at rho of about 0.85, which is what an imposed interface affinity predicts.

## Caveats

- The ring is real and survives a 1.5x grid when measured by signed distance to the contour: rim/core 1.88 and rim/out 3.05 at dx=1; 1.62-2.08 and 2.58-3.02 at dx=2/3. The 1.38-1.63 values the reviewers called 'at the gate' come from labkit's centroid-radial metric misreading rods. That metric is not why B fails.
- The construction auditor's 'rod-made s fails the gate (1.26)' uses that same flawed labkit metric. By signed distance the rod-made copy passes just barely (1.52 / 5.09), so whether the rods' own product could carry B is open. The case for by-construction rests on the imposed drift up 1 - phi^2, on the bath making most of the s, on far-bath-made s alone forming a ring of 2.18 / 2.16, and on the ring doing no work.
- M clears its gate only in the tail, and only generously under labkit's sqrt(area) body length (rods are about 1.46 sqrt(area) long). In long-axis lengths, 1-2 tracks per run reach 3. No track reaches 3 body lengths at 1.5x, where one founder gives about 13 long-lived tracks by t=600-800.
- No individual swims 3 or more body lengths and then divides; all_together_and_divides is empty on all 5 replication runs. Track 46 shows D only through its own birth by fission. Typical motion per cell cycle is about 1-2 body lengths, and several of the top movers shrink and dissolve.
- The parent's 'motility not dt-converged' limit, which c06's Weak verdict relied on, is an artifact of labkit's --dt flag, which only relabels time. A genuine dt/2 converges. The collective's numerical-validity record for this line should be corrected.
- Founding and division depend on resolution. At 1.5x only one nucleus forms, and long rods sometimes break by buckling into about 5 pieces instead of binary fission. This was seen on one seed only.
- Growth, and so repeated division, is fed by an imposed open chemostat kr(phi_b - phi). E is not claimed, but the same term is why the bath makes surfactant.

## Settling experiment proposed by the judge

**Goal.** Test whether the rods' own product, with no surfactant made by the medium, forms a ring that passes the gate across seeds and at 1.5x. That is the condition under which the ad3 quasi-equilibrium precedent would let B count. The test is cheap because s does not act back, so every arm has bit-identical bodies.

**Runs** (about 55-70 min on 2 threads):
1. The auditor's three-way source-split world, unchanged, which tracks rod-made (phi > 0), interface-made (-0.5 < phi <= 0) and far-bath-made (phi <= -0.5) copies of s in one run: /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-physim/a7c2ec48-e3a6-5436-88da-a7523dbbe428/scratchpad/audit/nematic-ringed/construction/worlds/world_srcsplit3.py. Run it with labkit at 128^2 and T=1000 on the claim's seeds 0, 1 and 2, about 8-10 min each.
2. One 1.5x-grid run: WORLD_P dx=0.6667, 192^2, seed 0, T=600, about 15 min.
3. One chis=0 twin of the rod-only source on seed 2, about 8 min.

**Measurements** for each channel, on render frames from t=100 on:
- Signed-distance rim/core and rim/out: rim |d| <= 0.5 px, core d <= -1.5, outside 2 <= d <= 4, using the auditor's rimshare.py or my /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-physim/a7c2ec48-e3a6-5436-88da-a7523dbbe428/scratchpad/judge/sd_channels.py.
- The fraction of individuals with both ratios at 1.5 or above.
- labkit radial.
- The ring on track 46 of seed 2 in the rod-made channel alone.

**Decision rule.**
- **Pass:** the rod-made copy passes both ratios at 1.5 or above in aggregate and in at least 2/3 of individuals, on 3 of 3 seeds and at 1.5x, and the chis=0 twin shows a diffuse fill. Then a world whose source is restricted to the droplets (src = ks*c*[phi > 0]) has a self-made ring under the ad3 precedent. It can be filed as an amended SIBMD claim, still flagged as a passive ring that does no work.
- **Fail:** the rod-made copy stays near 1.5 or below on any seed or at 1.5x, as seed 2 already suggests (1.52 by signed distance, 1.26 by labkit). Then B is by construction for this line, and C:swim-divide needs a ring that does work, such as gating a cargo species (the C:contained-swimmers route).
