# Q:bd-rtwo56-dog-twin: r-two56 against a Laplacian-generated (Gaussian) twin (c32)

world.py = threads/lenia-multichannel/worlds/r-two56/world.py with one switch. WORLD_P {"twin": "none"} is
the original. {"twin": "gauss"} replaces each of the 6 ring kernels K_i by the Gaussian heat kernel
exp(-k^2 tau_i) with tau_i = <r^2>_i / 4, i.e. the same mass (1) and second moment as K_i, so
U_i = exp(tau_i lap) A: the potential is generated by the Laplacian alone and keeps only K_i's first two
moments. Growth functions, weights, dt, initial noise patch and seeds are unchanged. "scale" multiplies tau.

Original: the noise patch breaks up into 4-8 isolated cogs (A0 core, A1 toothed shell) by t~30 that sit
still to t=100. Twin: the patch is a single body whose edge advances as a front with a two-layer
A1/A0 band at the interface and fills the torus; at no width does it break into individuals.
Note: under current labkit the original's shell is below the 1.5 rim/core gate in aggregate
(A1 1.31-1.40; 6-9 of 30-44 individuals membrane-like), so what the kernel demonstrably provides is
individuation (I), with the shell (B) itself borderline.
