## Nearest published work

- **Reacting thermals (c49, submitted).** Combustion theory already has self-sustained, moving burning pockets.
  Yanez, Kagan, Sivashinsky & Kuznetsov 2023 (Combust. Flame 258:113059; arXiv 2308.08537) model 2D *self-drifting
  flame balls* in Hele-Shaw cells with a constant-density, buoyancy-free reaction-diffusion model with heat loss: in 2D a
  non-drifting flame ball cannot exist, so drifting flamelets are the only 2D flame balls. Yanez et al. 2025 (Fire
  8:345) read the flamelets that a disintegrating near-limit front breaks into as Zeldovich flame balls stabilized by
  volumetric heat loss. Kagan, Yanez, Kuznetsov & Sivashinsky 2026 (Fire 9:74) add Boussinesq buoyancy to that model
  and find that buoyancy widens the propagation limits of upward flames. Experiments: Veiga-Lopez et al. 2020 (PRL
  124:174501): per the search excerpt (abstract page blocked, so UNVERIFIED beyond it) "flame cells split and bifurcate
  cyclically, with newborn flame ...". Kagan & Sivashinsky 1997, "Self-fragmentation of nonadiabatic cellular flames"
  (Combust. Flame 108:220) is cited in Yanez 2025's reference list (UNVERIFIED, not opened). **Relation: variant.**
  Splitting and drifting flamelets at low Lewis number with heat loss are known in combustion. What c49 adds, as far as
  these abstracts go, is a *population* on a regrowing fuel with tracked lineages (generation 5-11), buoyant caps that
  move 3+ lengths between their own birth and their own fission, and the g=0 twin showing that in this regime the flow,
  not the reaction-diffusion, supplies the motion. A reviewer should know the ledger's no-flow twin is itself a known
  self-replicating flame-spot system.
- **Phoretic swim-divide colloids (c33, established SI).** Soto & Golestanian 2014 (PRL 112:068301; arXiv 1306.6596):
  symmetrically coated catalytic colloids self-assemble into "molecules" that are inert or have spontaneous translation
  and spin, set by their composition and surface chemistry. **Relation: variant** for assembly from a gas plus
  motility; the division of composites into daughters that swim again is not in the abstract.
- **Selection for movement (C:minority-swimmers-spread, c45's Marangoni line).** Shine, Brown & Phillips 2011 (PNAS
  108:5708): traits that raise dispersal accumulate at expanding range edges ("spatial sorting"), an evolutionary
  process that works through space rather than through survival or reproduction. Perkins, Boettiger & Phillips 2016
  (Ecol. Evol. 6:6425): in a two-phenotype (fast/slow) model with diffusion, fast dispersers pre-empt distant sites
  (the "Olympic village" effect). Without a dispersal/life-history trade-off they keep an equilibrium frequency higher
  than their introduction frequency after expansion stops; with a trade-off, dispersal decays rapidly everywhere.
  **Relation:** the ledger's sink-escape environment (c45) rewards swimmers by survival; spatial sorting rewards them by
  *arrival* and has not been tried.
