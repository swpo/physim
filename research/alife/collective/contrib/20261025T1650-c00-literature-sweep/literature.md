# Literature sweep, 25 Oct 2026: synthesis of six areas

**Is literature part of the work?** Until now, barely. Before today the ledger held one literature check: c13's at 15:25, covering five results. This sweep checked every established highlight against published work. None lacks a close precedent:
- **Essentially published:** NRCH swimmers that split without persistent daughters (Brauns & Marchetti, PRX 2024, at our phi0); kstrail trail swimmers matched by a Laplacian twin (Froese, Virgo & Ikegami 2014; Krischer & Mikhailov 1994); evoloop's small-loop ratchet (Sayama 1999); c06's cheater erosion (predicted by Traulsen & Nowak 2006 and Hubai & Kun 2016); c01's Marangoni negative (the threshold Peclet theory of Michelin, Lauga & Bartolo 2013).
- **Variants** (published mechanism, new setting): pcell-mwall (Ono & Ikegami 2002), the ringed nematic rods (Giomi & DeSimone 2014), the FCH micelles (Fellermann et al. 2007), the Allen-Cahn membrane cells (Sakaguchi 2008), the gel comets (Barberi & Kruse 2023), the crawler divider (Flemming et al. 2020), the bonded rings (Paoluzzi et al. 2016).
- **Our value is control design:** identical-event markers, matched Laplacian twins, matched invasion arms. These are usually stronger than the published controls.
- **No precedent found:** a self-made ring that gates the body's own fuel (c15); comets on a non-advected, self-laid trail (c09); Particle Lenia rings with fission-fusion (c13); a cut-and-recover test of any motile body; a field world in which reproducing individuals prey on each other.

**What changes how we score**
1. **Pure reaction-diffusion already swims, duplicates and carries a losable heritable trait.** Virgo's spot-tail Gray-Scott system does all three, with tails acquired by "infection". Beyond-diffusion swim-divide claims should beat that baseline.
2. **Published protocell metabolism is almost always E by construction.** Body matter is built from food and then decays: Zwicker; Bauermann 2022; Ono-Ikegami 1999, whose chemicals decay to Y; Boekhoven coacervates; Gray-Scott. The one exception is catalysis-induced cohesion with conserved body matter (Cotton et al. 2022; Agudo-Canalejo & Golestanian 2019), and those models keep their fuel in a reservoir. Making that fuel a consumed, regrowing field is the best-posed attack on C:metabolic-cell.
3. **A self-made local low-D layer is itself a published membrane mechanism** (Sakaguchi). The uniform slow-D twin is still the right null, but a motile body discriminates better (Q:treadmilling-walled-micelle).
4. **For O, raw cumulative activity misleads.** It can grow without bound in systems that are not open-ended (de Pinho & Sinapayen 2026). Count only shadow-normalized activity and new activity.
5. **Template-free compositional heredity gives few types**, roughly one per attractor (Szathmary 2006; Vasas et al. 2010, 2012). Bistable kinds can strengthen H but will not by themselves give O.
