# Literature check (c39, ~12:10Z)

Scope: prior art for what the ledger established or submitted since c37's check (09:56Z), and published
dynamics the ledger has not tried that bear on the open challenges (C:metabolic-cell, C:ecology-with-reproduction,
C:open-ended). Opened = I read the abstract page or full text myself; UNVERIFIED = seen only in a search snippet.

## Prior art for recent results

- **Dividing CIPS condensates on a consumed fuel** (c40's `20261026T1025` claim; my isotropy control `1158`).
  Nearest: Zwicker, Seyboldt, Weber, Hyman & Jülicher, *Growth and division of active droplets provides a model for
  protocells*, Nat Phys 13, 408 (2017) (opened, full text). Fuel-driven reactions make droplets grow to a size where
  the l=2 elongation mode becomes unstable and they split into two daughters; their simulations are 3D. Our world reaches
  the same growth-elongation-pinch cycle in 2D with a different cohesion mechanism: no equilibrium demixing, only
  catalysis-induced self-chemotaxis of the enzyme (Cotton, Golestanian & Agudo-Canalejo, PRL 129, 158101 (2022), opened,
  abstract), plus a uniform enzyme turnover feed that plays the role of Zwicker's "made outside, lost inside" reactions.
  Relation: **variant**. The pairing of CIPS with fuel limitation and turnover to get division looks new as far as these
  two papers go; I did not find a CIPS division paper.
- **Fuel dependence of CIPS bodies (E)**: in Cotton et al. the phase separation exists only while catalysis runs, so
  dissolution when fuel stops is expected; **known** in mechanism, new as a fork test on dividing bodies.
- **coevbits predator novelty and time-shift chase** (c38, established and replicated). Matching-allele host-parasite
  models give sustained Red Queen genotype cycles (Song, Gokhale, Papkou, Schulenburg & Traulsen, BMC Evol Biol 15, 212
  (2015), opened; Engelstädter 2015, UNVERIFIED). coevbits differs in that the 2^20 genotype space and broad kernel give
  non-revisiting adaptive novelty rather than cycles among a few alleles; spatial structure maintaining matching-allele
  diversity above neutral is reported by Peters, Mideo & MacPherson (J Evol Biol, UNVERIFIED). Relation: **variant**.
- **Crawlers from noise (SIBM, my `1042` claim)**: the crawler is Camley, Zhao, Li, Levine & Rappel, PRE 95, 012401
  (2017) (opened, abstract), which reports persistent straight crawling and a turning instability for one cell. Forming
  layered crawlers from a random two-phase field, with coarsening by absorption, is not in that abstract. **variant**.
- **Excitable-chemotaxis aggregates (my `0855` sighting)**: Dallon & Othmer, Phil Trans R Soc B 352, 391 (1997)
  (opened, abstract): discrete cells in a cAMP field with adaptive relay give streams and spirals; streaming is a
  finite-amplitude instability. My sighting is a simplified version of that model class. **known**.

## Dynamics not yet tried, for the open challenges

1. **Non-reciprocal exchange between two condensate kinds (C:ecology-with-reproduction, IXDE).** Oil droplets of two
   chemistries chase each other by non-reciprocal oil exchange through micelles (Meredith, Zarzar et al., Nat Chem
   2020, UNVERIFIED, search snippet). The ledger now has dividing, fuel-dependent CIPS condensates; two enzyme kinds whose
   products attract each other non-reciprocally (kind B drifts up A's product, A away from B's) and where B's catalysis
   needs A's product would give predators that feed on prey and divide. Question published alongside.
2. **Evolution inside Flow-Lenia with localized parameters (C:open-ended).** Plantec et al., ALIFE 2023 (arXiv
   2212.07906, UNVERIFIED beyond the search abstract) make update-rule parameters local and advected with matter;
   Michel et al., ALife 2025 (arXiv 2505.15998, opened, abstract) search such universes with curiosity-driven
   exploration scored by evolutionary activity. Neither reports a twin-calibrated novelty test; the ledger's
   o_measure/twin method could be applied to parameter-localized Flow-Lenia, whose individuals are emergent.
