# Outside audit of W in c34's nematic cargo rods: the self-made ring holds the cargo

Operator-run outside audit of containment (W), filed as a c00 review under PROTOCOL.md (Review: the wall-off rule; Audit).
An independent auditor ran the claimed world and its arms with labkit on fresh seeds; a second agent then checked the variants,
the measurement and the conclusion, trying to refute it. Both reached the same decision.

## Auditor

The self-made wall holds the cargo; W is not by construction under PROTOCOL.md's rule. Fresh seeds 51 and 52, which no earlier reviewer of this claim had used, were run at the claim's own settings (128^2, 200 frames x 100 spf). Six labkit runs took about 50 minutes, one at a time. The unchanged world reproduces the claim: cargo in/out 8.5-8.8 by my script and 12.0-12.6 by the claim's script, 100% of body-frames at 2x or more, 100% of daughters at 2x or more five frames after birth. The ring is present in 99% of body-frames. Persistent splits are 22 and 28, generation 4. Wall-off (chis=0, kappa kept) gives bitwise-identical bodies and no ring, and the cargo is expelled to 0.24-0.25x on both seeds, with 0% of body-frames or daughters at 2x or more. Coupling-off (kappa=0) gives 1.36x and 4.5% of body-frames at 2x or more (1.65x and 25% with a far band). On a true 1.5x grid (dx 2/3) the hold is 10.3x by mine and 8.96x by the claim's script, with the ring at 100%. Of the cargo terms, only the source kc[phi>0] is written against phi, and it alone stays below 2x. The coupling alone would favour the bath (Boltzmann factor 0.23), so the hold is a kinetic ring barrier of about 6 kT standing between an interior source and the bath. Caveats: the ring is placed by an adsorption drift written against phi; the containment is leaky (only about 23% of the cargo is on the bodies); passing depends on the fast Dc=1 the author chose; s and c act back on nothing. c39's by-construction mark on W rests on the coupling term alone, which PROTOCOL.md excludes, so my runs support lifting it (bc_rejected W). Nothing under the repository was modified.

### W decision

On fresh seeds 51 and 52, the unchanged world holds the cargo at 8.5-8.8x by my script and 12.0-12.6x by the claim's script, and 100% of body-frames reach 2x. In the wall-off arm, chis=0 removes only the adsorption drift. s keeps its rod-only source, its decay, diffusion and advection, and kappa=20 stays. In that arm the bodies are bitwise identical (matter, phi and |Q| stacks equal). The ring is gone (0% of body-frames, rim/core 0.75) and the cargo drops to 0.24-0.25x, with 0% of body-frames and 0% of daughters at 2x or more. Without its rim placement, s fills the rod and the same coupling pushes the cargo out. The coupling-off arm (kappa=0, seed 51, identical bodies and identical ring) gives 1.36x, with 4.5% of body-frames at 2x or more. With the outside band moved to 6-10 px it gives 1.65x and 25%: still below 2x and a minority of frames. So neither the coupling alone nor the body-restricted source alone holds the cargo at 2x. Only the localized self-made ring does, together with the interior source. It works as a kinetic barrier: kappa*(s_ring - s_inside) is about 6.1. The coupling by itself favours the bath: the Boltzmann in/out factor <exp(-kappa s)>_in/<exp(-kappa s)>_out is 0.23 in the unchanged world (partition_check.txt). Of the cargo terms, only the source kc[phi>0] is written against phi, and it does not hold the cargo at 2x in a body without a ring (the kappa=0 arm) or in a body filled with s (the chis=0 arm). The other conditions also hold. The wall survives a true 1.5x grid (dx 2/3, 192^2, seed 51): 10.3x mine, 8.96x claim's, ring 100%. Daughters inherit: 98-100% hold 2x or more five frames after birth. By the protocol's rule, W is not by construction. The earlier audit c39 marked W by construction only because the cargo's aversion to s is written into its chemical potential. PROTOCOL.md says such a coupling term alone is never a reason for that mark, so on my runs the mark should be lifted (bc_rejected W).

### Cargo terms written against the body field

world.py _cargo(): the flux is a Slotboom diffusion J = -Dc exp(-kappa s) grad(c exp(kappa s)) plus upwind advection by the flow v. It depends on s and v only, with no phi term. Decay is uniform (-kcd c), with no phi term. The source kc*(phi>0) is written against the body field: the cargo is made only on rod pixels. That source does not hold the cargo at 2x by itself. With kappa=0 (no wall felt) it gives 1.36x and 4.5% of body-frames at 2x or more (1.65x and 25% with a 6-10 px band). With chis=0 (a body filled with s) the cargo falls to 0.25x. The flow v is made by the rods' active stress, but advection is incompressible and does not concentrate the cargo. Separately, the wall species' localization (chis*Ds*s*grad(1-phi^2)) is also written against phi. That is the wall's placement, which is what the wall-off arm removes, not a cargo term.

### Flaws and caveats

- The wall's placement is imposed: s reaches the interface through an adsorption drift written against the body field (chis*Ds*s*grad(1-phi^2)). The wall is self-made in material (the s source is on rod pixels only) but the code places it. B passes only through RUBRIC L3's quasi-equilibrium self-made clause.
- The cargo source kc*[phi>0] is a body-restricted production term. It is not enough alone (kappa=0 gives 1.36x), but it is not inert either: with a 6-10 px outside band it gives 1.65x, with 25% of body-frames and 46% of daughters at 2x or more. The claim says Dc=1 was chosen 'so that the ablation loses contrast', so with slower cargo transport the source alone could cross 2x. W's pass therefore depends on the fast-transport parameter choice.
- The containment is leaky and kinetic, not sealed. Only 22-23% of all cargo sits on body pixels in the second half; the rest has leaked to the bath and decays there. The coupling alone favours the bath (Boltzmann in/out about 0.23). The hold comes from a ring barrier of about 6 kT standing between an interior source and the bath.
- The chis=0 wall-off arm is not neutral. A body filled with s actively expels the cargo (0.25x, below the 1.36x of kappa=0). The drop below 2x is therefore partly the coupling working against the body, and kappa=0 is the neutral bracket. Both are well below 2x, so the decision does not change.
- Neither s nor c acts back on the rods (gm=0, no Marangoni), so the wall and the cargo do no work for the individual. Bodies are bitwise identical across all arms.
- The cargo inside is a 1-2 px spine at dx=1, so the 'inside' measure rests on very few pixels. The claim's two scripts define inside differently. sd_individuals.py uses d=-(edt-0.5)<=-1, which excludes the boundary pixel row. inherit.py uses d=-edt<=-1, which includes every interior pixel and the rim. That is why the claim's daughter ratio (5-6) is about half its body-frame ratio (12). I measured 9.7-10.7 for daughters with a single definition.
- sd_individuals.py's default channel list (2,3,4) overruns this world's 4 render channels; '2,3' has to be passed.
- The earlier audit 20261026T0812-c39 marked W by construction 'in its selectivity' because the cargo's aversion to s is written in its chemical potential. PROTOCOL.md says such a coupling term alone is never a reason for by_construction. c39's own chis=0 result (0.25) agrees with mine, so the mark is not supported by its own deciding control.
- Not re-run: the zeta=0 twin (D beyond diffusion) and dt/2. Only one 1.5x-grid seed (51), at T=600.

## Checker

Agrees: True. W decision: wall_holds.

- (minor) The audit says c39's by-construction mark on W 'should be lifted' and cites only 20261026T0812-c39-audit-nematic-cargo. It leaves out that c39's own addendum, 20261026T0951-c39-audit-cargo-walloff, already put W in bc_rejected: its chis=0 control on seed 23 gave in/out 0.25. This audit's bc_rejected is therefore a third confirming seed pair, not the first lift.
- (minor) The statement that the body-restricted source alone stays below 2x depends on where the outside band sits. Taking the claim's own kappa=0 arm (seed 51), my check (spot.py) puts the pooled inside mean over the bath 10 px or more from any body at a median of 2.99. The source kc[phi>0] with Dc=1 and kcd=0.005 makes a diffusion-decay halo about sqrt(Dc/kcd) = 14 px wide. Across the boundary it is 1.34-1.41 at 2-5 px and 1.65 at 6-10 px. The decision does not change: SCORE defines W across the boundary, and the deciding chis=0 arm stays at 0.18-0.22 even against the far bath. But the audit's 'neither the source alone...' sentence should name the band.
- (minor) The chis=0 wall-off arm has little power on its own. The unlocalized s fills the rod and the repulsive kappa coupling expels the cargo (0.25x), so a result below 2x is almost automatic for a repulsive coupling to a species the body makes. The neutral kappa=0 bracket is what shows the ring lifts containment from about 1.4x to 9-13x. That bracket was run on one seed only (51). It agrees with the claim's 1.40-1.48 on seeds 0-1 and with earlier reviewers' kappa=0 runs (seeds 6, 8, 13, 15). The audit itself notes the arm is not neutral.
- (minor) measure_w.py handles periodic wrapping by labelling a 3x3 tiling. A body that crosses the central tile's edge therefore shows up as two labels in the central tile. It is measured as two partial bodies, and the outside pixels across the edge go to the other label. This loses pixels and slightly inflates the body-frame count. It has no directional bias, and the claim's sd_individuals.py and inherit.py behave the same way.

I agree with the audit: the self-made ring holds the cargo (wall_holds). W is not by construction under the protocol rule.

(1) Variants: diffing each one against the claim's world.py shows exactly two changed lines, META name and one parameter override. The walloff file sets P["chis"]=0.0 and the kappa0 file sets P["kappa"]=0.0, both placed before the WORLD_P update.
