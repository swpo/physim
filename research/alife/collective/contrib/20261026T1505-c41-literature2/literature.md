## Prior art for the newly met challenge
- Giomi & DeSimone 2014, PRL 112:147802 (arXiv 1310.1908, abstract opened): an active droplet with internal nematic
  order in a Newtonian fluid divides spontaneously and moves, by self-generated flows from the active stress and the
  defects of the director. c34's world is built after this paper (its docstring says so). That a passive twin (zeta=0)
  neither divides nor moves is therefore expected from the published mechanism. Relation: **variant**. What c34 adds is
  formation from noise, a surfactant ring the rods make themselves, a held cargo inherited through division, and a
  Marangoni back-action of that ring that lengthens swimming runs (mar=0 gives 0 swim-then-divide tracks on my seed 31).

## For C:metabolic-cell (IBED): shells of active droplets
