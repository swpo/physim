# How information moved in the first lab

A retrospective from the record (reviews, cross-pollination log, digest, notebooks, world META parents and git history), written before the collective started. It motivated the collective's design: one shared, continuously rebuilt state that every agent reads, instead of per-thread review feedback.

## Verdict

Partly, and mostly in one burst. The 16:36 digest gave every researcher the other six report paths and a 'fork one mechanism from another thread' challenge, and 15 of the lab's 22 cross-thread forks (in all 7 threads) were committed before the thread's own sprint-1 review reached it. After that, ideas crossed threads only inside each thread's own director message, and only when the director wrote them into that thread's commissioned experiment: 7 more forks, mostly method imports (identical-event twins, ledgers, matched neutral arms). The 89 cross-thread cross-pollination suggestions were a log, not a delivery channel: 13 were acted on, every one of them carried by the target's own review; none of the ~36 that never reached the target's messages was acted on; and 12 r2 plus all 34 r3 suggestions were written after the target's last working-sprint review. So results did map back to every agent once (every thread imported something, and imports were ingredients of the sprint-2/3 headline work of six threads, all but particle-life, though only in active-droplets was the import a load-bearing mechanism), but there was no second aggregation, and from mid-sprint 2 each thread mostly ran on its own review, one review cycle (2-3 h) behind the others.

## Lessons

- One synchronous broadcast with an explicit fork challenge and branch paths moved more ideas than 89 targeted suggestions: 15 of 22 forks followed the 16:36 digest. Broadcast after every round, not once.
- A log at the source is not delivery. Every adopted suggestion arrived inside the target's own review (13/13), and none of the ~36 that never reached the target's messages was acted on. Route each suggestion into the target's inbox or next brief with a delivered timestamp, and have the next review check an adoption status.
- Order matters. 12 r1 suggestions waited a full cycle, 12 r2 suggestions and all 34 r3 ones arrived after the target's last working review, and the committee still marked fisim as 'not acting on' two suggestions it never received. Only grade adoption of items that were delivered.
- Batch review latency, not willingness, was the bottleneck. r1 reviews queued up to 82 min and reached 3 threads after their sprint-2 work was done, so ideas crossed threads once per review cycle (2-3 h; fisim's twins reached 4 threads 2h18-2h55 after they existed). Use a continuously updated shared state the agents sync from, plus a short blocking triage.
- Methods transferred better than mechanisms. Identical-event twins, confined alleles and conversion ledgers each reached 4-5 threads; mechanism transplants paid off once (fisim gate into AD's inert shell, which had a slot waiting for it) and failed or stayed neutral 6 times (trail propulsion, hunters, repellent droplets, maintenance in fisim, particle-life turnover, food gate). Advertise reusable methods and say what slot a mechanism needs.
- The committee's commissioned main experiments were the most reliable carrier in sprint 3 (every sprint-3 import came that way). Pointers to tools were not: kindtrack.py and fission.py were pointed to 9 times, reused 0 times, and every thread wrote its own buggy tracker. Ship shared tools as a library, not as branch paths.
- Exchange was lopsided. AD, autopoiesis and fisim exported, flow-lenia and particle-life exported nothing forked, and particle-life's one fork was switched off. A digest of headlines favours flashy mechanisms; also surface negative laws (selection needs turnover, coverage, the dt trap), which travelled only through director messages.
- Broadcast only reviewed claims. DIGEST_r1 spread an L7 claim and a generation count that were withdrawn within 1-2 h.
- Credit is incomplete. sporecell3's twin design and flow-lenia gene2 use fisim's method without naming it in META.parent, and fl_predator credits no source. Make cross-thread provenance a required, checked field.

## Broadcasts

- 14:25 UTC: setup push to the lab branch (labkit v0.1, rubric v1, CHARTER, seven BRIEFs; LITERATURE.md 14:52). No results yet. The charter asked researchers to record forks from other threads in META.parent and said the director would 'point out what other threads found'; the autopoiesis brief asked for 'a mechanism others can transplant'.
- 16:36 UTC: DIGEST_r1, the only results broadcast, sent as a message to all seven researchers and committed as committee/DIGEST_r1.md. It carried: (1) one paragraph per thread on sprint 1, with the branch path of every REPORT_r1; (2) three convergences: gated mobility at a boundary as the transferable containment operator, a consumed regrowing resource plus a maintenance cost, and the L7 bar (in-run variation, matched neutral, >=3 seeds); (3) the sprint-2 challenge to fork one mechanism from another thread into META.parent/mutation, aim at motile or interacting heritable individuals, and make one striking movie; (4) logistics. It was written from unreviewed reports: it said autopoiesis and AD had met the L7 bar and particle-life reached 3-9 generations, and the autopoiesis L7 and the particle-life generations were later withdrawn. Six notebooks log reading it at 16:36-16:37; particle-life cites its 'lab convergence #1'.
- 17:47 UTC: rubric v2 and RUBRIC_CHANGES.md pushed to the lab branch, not sent as a message. Every rule names a counterexample from a specific thread, so it indirectly relayed other threads' failures. It reached researchers through lab-branch merges and their r2 reviews.
- 17:57-19:40 UTC: labkit v0.2 pushed to the lab branch (radial boundary profiles, absolute lineage window with a merge filter, mature-cell damage targeting, species tracking). Threads merged it at sprint-3 start; infrastructure, not ideas.
- 16:54-21:47 UTC (passive, not a broadcast): CROSS_POLLINATION.md and LEADERBOARD.md were updated on the lab branch with every review. Each thread merged the lab branch at least 3 times, so the files sat in their working trees, but no NOTEBOOK, REPORT or FINAL cites reading either.
- None after 16:36: there was no sprint-2 digest. From then on all 21 reviews (plus 2 discarded re-runs) were per-thread messages.
