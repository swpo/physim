# Organism evaluation rubric v0.1

Effective 23 October 2026. The target is a localized individual with distinguishing features that persist with it across space and time. Replication means copying those features and comes later, followed by selection. This is a research rubric for organism-like candidates, not a definition of life.

## Three essential gates

Judge each gate **supported**, **contradicted**, or **insufficient evidence**, with an actual observation and a limitation. Promotion to an organism-like candidate requires support for all three. There is no compensating total score.

| Gate | Evidence required | Useful quantitative diagnostics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. A localized, coherent individual** | A discernible entity with a boundary or coherent inside/outside organization, distinguishable from the surrounding world. A threshold contour alone does not establish a membrane. | Area, span, domain fraction, true enclosed interiors, local enclosing-wall fraction, exterior occupancy and sensitivity to thresholds. Record growth and fragmentation. |
| **2. A distinguishing, nontrivial feature** | A specified spatial arrangement or functional organization belonging to the individual: for example, a differentiated boundary/interior or persistent internal pattern. A round outline, splitting, a color assigned by the renderer, or arbitrary soup asymmetry alone is insufficient. Round shape does not disqualify an otherwise organized individual. | Individual channel maps and boundary/interior/exterior profiles; a specified feature descriptor or functional readout. Report what the quantity measures. Entropy, channel count or a scalar field mean alone does not establish organization. |
| **3. Persistence of that feature with that individual** | Follow the named individual and feature through the declared observation window, including the end. Distinguish continued organization from disappearance, replacement, merging or splitting. A feature may change shape while its organization persists. | Identified observations/retained observations, feature-supported intervals, longest unbroken observed interval, identity ambiguities and event brackets. Record simulation times, cadence, missing fields and censored intervals. |

State the proposed feature before a new test, the relevant formation/relaxation timescale when known, and why the observation window is meaningful. Duration and descriptor thresholds remain provisional until calibrated; do not turn the current examples' 100–1000 simulation units into a universal rule. Sampled continuity cannot exclude events between saved states.

## Strength of the interpretation

Report active maintenance, causal functional dependence, perturbation recovery, source dependence and initialization separately. These strengthen or weaken a biological interpretation; they are not an extra universal gate. A known passive or externally imposed template must be labeled as such and cannot be promoted as a self-maintaining organism. Missing perturbation evidence means maintenance is untested. Apparent complexity does not demonstrate autonomy.

The rubric is independent of the world model. Diffusion-based equations are one construction, not a requirement. An alternative must state its predicted advantage; record its model/version and numerical validity under the same three gates.

## Quantitative anchor and loopholes

The existing [compartment detector](legacy-enclosure-method.md) supplies morphology diagnostics: true interiors without artificial closing or convex hulls; five normalized thresholds (0.25–0.65); agreement at at least three; and local wall fraction at least 0.5. These are **legacy provisional screens**, not calibrated biological thresholds. Threshold agreement is not temporal persistence. Keep exterior occupancy and the other diagnostics separate; the old aggregate score is not an organism score.

- Check checkerboards and crowded network pockets against true interiors and local walls. A globally busy world does not automatically disqualify a coherent individual.
- Empty rings and stationary blobs need a demonstrated distinguishing feature. State any injected or inherited template and what later dynamics actually establish.
- Show the full declared time window and surrounding context. Do not choose a peak frame, favorable crop or threshold after seeing failure.
- Track individuals, not pooled population asymmetry or counts. Mark split/merge ambiguity; do not silently assign a parent's identity or feature to a child.
- Treat numerical failures and rendering artifacts as invalid evidence, and never as biological novelty.

## Calibration, changes and stopping

The [initial calibration](2026-10-23.md) uses real retained observations and includes weak comparisons. It contains no established biological positive; quantitative cutoffs are therefore provisional.

Freeze this version for an admitted experiment. A revision must name the observed counterexample or loophole, explain the change, and compare old and new judgments on the same frozen examples. Preserve prior verdicts and the version used. Investigators may challenge a judgment with a concrete trajectory or missing observation; disagreement alone is not a new cutoff. Require human input when a proposed change alters the mission itself.

Before another study, name the missing gate, competing predictions, smallest discriminating evidence and decision for each outcome. Prefer a bounded review of saved evidence when it can answer the question. After that review, conclude, promote or reprioritize; do not automatically retune or start replication work. A study unable to change an essential gate or a material interpretation should not take priority merely because it is ready to run.
