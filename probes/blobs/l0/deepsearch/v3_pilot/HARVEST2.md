# V3 continuation — audited final harvest

Two islands completed generation12 and all final confirmations. Full checkpoints
and six GPU re-simulation films are secured locally; both campaign pods are
terminated. Raw simulation data were preserved, not repaired or re-scored.

## Measured result

At common W9=0.40, the continuation has159 qualifying creative screens out of723,
versus241/1376 in generations1–7. Qualifying means status=ok,C9>=0.4 and common
interest>=60. The continuation observed45 qualifying descriptor bins, including
14 not seen qualifying in the baseline. These are MAP-Elites bins, NOT physical
cells or proof of heterogeneous compartments. Partial/full C9 are separate assays;
the detailed tables split them and do not impute missing C9 as zero.

The best observed correctly linked partial-C9 selected assay increased from
0.8504 to0.8895 (p1g12_049_s3, add_chan origin). The same genotype scored
C9=0.6737/0.8854/0.8895 across its screen/seed2/seed3 observations. Screen means
improved descriptively, but selected-confirmation yields did not uniformly
improve. Multiple settings changed together; this is not a causal comparison or
a test of an unrun same-configuration continuation.

## Corrections to earlier claims

- The old0.82 SIC ratio pooled52 qualifying screens and40 confirmation rows over
 112 screen candidates. Direct screen yield was52/112=46.4%. Same-genome reseeds
 are separate, adaptively selected observations, not IID trials.
- SIC screens use spatially composed ICs; their confirmation jobs use ordinary
 soup initial conditions. Same-genome confirmation does not replicate the
 composed spatial state.
- Eleven older third-seed jobs used a same-named donor-v2 genome rather than the
 named v3screen genotype. They are excluded from strict replication claims.
 All389 continuation selected assays match their named screen physical model.
- The initial archive reblend made29 wrong-physical-genome joins. Candidate
 names were not globally unique, and name-based best-row joins were unsafe.
 Numeric reblend consistency does not establish provenance consistency.
- One C9-backfill identity has two different genomes; both variants are retained
 but quarantined from primary summaries. All2324 baseline rows are preserved.
- h9v0 is not a validated compartment-diversity ranker. Toy counterexamples
 invalidate the earlier ceiling/absence/homogenization claims. Do not deploy
 it in evolution or select a biological cutoff without redesign/calibration.
