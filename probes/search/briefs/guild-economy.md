# Direction: GUILD ECONOMY (cross-feeding division of labor)

Extend the E2 enzyme-economics idea by ONE gene axis and get emergent
SPECIALIZATION. Cells carry allocation genome (a, 1-a): fraction a of enzyme
budget eats raw resource R (producing waste W as byproduct), fraction 1-a
eats waste W. Linear prices only (E2 style): income_R = cR*a*g_tot*V*R,
income_W = cW*(1-a)*g_tot*V*W; rent m0 + m1*g_tot; finite larder; bankruptcy
burns tissue; copy inheritance + mutation on a.

Target hierarchy:
  L1 (fast): local eating/waste diffusion
  L2 (medium): guild formation — bimodal allocation distribution (producers
      a~1 vs recyclers a~0), spatial interleaving (recyclers ring producers)
  L3 (slow, TOP): guild-ratio macro law — producer:recycler biomass ratio
      relaxes to a PRICE-DETERMINED equilibrium (marginal-return equalization
      — an emergent "market clearing" law!) after any perturbation.

Theory coordinates: price ratio cW/cR, waste yield per unit eaten, waste
decay rate, mutation size. G2 top model: relaxation (tau of guild-ratio
recovery); G3: equilibrium ratio must track cW/cR smoothly (the demand
curve is the top law!). Check guild bimodality with a dip test or histogram
valley depth. Beware density compensation (E2 lesson: need viability floor +
bankruptcy, not smooth shrink).

Real-world analogue: microbial cross-feeding / syntrophy; market clearing.
